# Writing Quality Ontology

Every concept in the Writing Quality Ontology has an entry here. Each entry includes a definition, multi-dimensional classification across all 13 dimensions, typed relationships to other concepts, and diagnostic guidance.

## All Concepts

### Mechanical layer (correctness)
- [[nominalization]] — verb-to-noun conversion; increases cognitive load and obscures agency
- [[passive-voice]] — agent-absent construction; risk factor for coherence failure
- [[lard-factor]] — ratio of deadwood to total words; measure of sentence compression
- [[weak-words]] — verbs and modifiers with low semantic content
- [[grammar-syntax]] — correctness of grammatical structures and syntactic relationships
- [[punctuation]] — rule-governed marking of sentence boundaries and relationships
- [[orthography]] — spelling, capitalization, and other writing conventions
- [[readability-metrics]] — computational proxies for reading difficulty (Flesch-Kincaid, etc.)
- [[e-prime]] — writing without any form of "to be"; philosophical constraint

### Structural layer (logic and architecture)
- [[coherence]] — logical continuity across sentences and sections
- [[cohesion]] — surface-level textual connection (pronouns, transitions, parallelism)
- [[unity]] — every element of a section serving a single controlling purpose
- [[boundary-control]] — discipline over what belongs in each unit of text
- [[rhetorical-arrangement]] — organizational strategy matching content to purpose
- [[rhetorical-mode]] — the structural pattern governing a passage (narration, description, etc.)
- [[informal-fallacy]] — logical error in argument structure
- [[hierarchy-of-purpose]] — the ordered set of goals a document is meant to accomplish

### Functional layer (purpose and audience)
- [[audience-awareness]] — calibrating all choices to the intended reader
- [[genre]] — the form-convention contract between writer and reader
- [[topic-type]] — the Diataxis-style classification of what a unit of content does

### Computational layer (pipelines and systems)
- [[tokenomics]] — token efficiency in AI language model contexts
- [[chunking]] — structuring content for retrieval and segmentation
- [[asset-validity]] — integrity of links, images, and other external dependencies
- [[controlled-vocabulary]] — an authoritative term set governing a documentation system

### Editorial layer (convention and appropriateness)
- [[house-style]] — codified conventions for a specific publication or organization
- [[tone-voice-cadence]] — the affective and rhythmic register of prose
- [[information-design]] — visual and spatial arrangement of content for access
- [[localization]] — adapting content for specific cultural, linguistic, or regional contexts
- [[terminology-consistency]] — using terms uniformly and deliberately throughout a document

### Cross-cutting
- [[plain-language]] — a multi-layer philosophy of writing for the reader's ease, not the writer's display
- [[paramedic-method]] — Richard Lanham's diagnostic procedure for identifying and restructuring weak prose

## Supporting Concepts

Outcomes, processes, and measurement frameworks referenced by the primary WQO concepts above.

### Outcomes (what writing quality affects)
- [[reader-comprehension]] — the degree to which a reader extracts intended meaning
- [[reader-trust]] — the reader's confidence that a text is accurate and produced in good faith
- [[readability]] — the aggregate ease with which a text can be processed and understood
- [[coherence-failure]] — the breakdown of logical continuity; what poor coherence produces
- [[accessibility-presentation]] — structural and navigational presentation for assistive technologies
- [[inclusivity]] — the property of not excluding readers based on identity, background, or ability

### Processes
- [[mechanical-editing]] — the sentence-level correction pass: grammar, punctuation, spelling
- [[rag-retrieval]] — how writing structure determines quality in retrieval-augmented generation pipelines

### Writing techniques
- [[active-voice]] — grammatical construction naming agents as subjects
- [[active-voice-prose]] — prose in which active constructions predominate; output of paramedic editing
- [[sentence-efficiency]] — ratio of meaningful content to total words
- [[word-level-error]] — localized lexical or morphological failure (misspelling, wrong word form)

### Readability metrics
- [[flesch-kincaid-score]] — reading ease and grade-level estimates from sentence and word length
- [[gunning-fog-index]] — years-of-education estimate based on sentence length and polysyllabic word proportion
