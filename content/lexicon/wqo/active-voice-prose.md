# Active Voice Prose

Active voice prose is the output of deliberate editing toward agency and directness. It is the positive outcome of applying the [[paramedic-method]] and revising away [[nominalization]] and [[passive-voice|passive constructions]]: prose in which something always does something, and the reader never has to infer who is responsible for what.

The term is a stylistic description rather than a grammatical rule. Not every sentence in active voice prose is grammatically active — but the overall effect is of forward motion, clear causation, and responsible agents. This contrasts with the bureaucratic or academic default of noun-heavy, passive-saturated prose in which events occur without actors and conclusions emerge without anyone concluding them.

[[lard-factor]] tends to be low in active voice prose: the compression that comes from naming agents directly and using strong verbs reduces word count as a byproduct of improving clarity.
