# Active Voice

In active voice, the grammatical subject is the agent: "The editor revised the manuscript." In [[passive-voice|passive voice]], the agent is demoted or absent: "The manuscript was revised." Active voice keeps agency on the surface, making causation and responsibility legible sentence by sentence.

Active voice is not universally superior — passive voice is appropriate when the agent is unknown, irrelevant, or deliberately withheld. Scientific writing, for example, conventionally uses passive voice to foreground the experimental result over the researcher's action. The WQO concern is not to eliminate passive voice but to deploy it deliberately rather than as a default.

[[e-prime]] is an extreme constraint that eliminates all forms of "to be," which often produces active constructions as a byproduct but operates on different grounds — philosophical rather than stylistic.

The [[paramedic-method]] uses active voice restoration as its primary diagnostic move: find the action buried in a nominalization or passive construction, make it the verb, and make its agent the subject.
