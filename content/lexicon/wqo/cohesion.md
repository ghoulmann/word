# Cohesion

Cohesion describes the surface-level linguistic devices that signal connection between sentences and across a text. Where [[coherence]] is about whether the logic holds together, cohesion is about whether the *surface* of the text signals that connection explicitly.

## Devices of Cohesion

**Pronominal reference**: using pronouns to carry a noun across sentences — "The committee approved the proposal. *It* had been in review for three months." The "it" connects to "proposal" without restating it.

**Transitional expressions**: "therefore," "however," "consequently," "in addition," "by contrast" — these signal the logical relationship between claims at the sentence boundary.

**Lexical repetition and synonymy**: restating a key term or using a near-synonym ("the system," then "the software," then "this platform") to maintain the thread.

**Parallel structure**: presenting related items in the same grammatical form signals their relationship without stating it: "We researched the problem, analyzed the options, and chose the best solution."

**Given-new structure**: beginning each sentence with information already established (given) before introducing new information maintains a chain that readers can follow.

## Cohesion Without Coherence

A text can be highly cohesive — every sentence connected by transitions, every noun tracked with pronouns — while being logically incoherent. "However, the system was implemented. Furthermore, the results were analyzed. Consequently, the evaluation was completed." — cohesive surface, no logical argument.

Cohesion devices can become a compensatory habit when the underlying logical structure is unclear: excessive use of transitional phrases often signals that the writer is trying to *signal* connection that isn't actually there.

## When Cohesion Failures Matter

Low cohesion is a readability problem at the sentence and paragraph scale. It becomes a structural problem when the absence of connecting devices forces the reader to infer all logical relationships — the cognitive load increases enough to interrupt reading.

Cohesion is addressed during editing, after the structural argument (coherence) is stable. Adding cohesion devices to structurally incoherent prose is cosmetic rather than corrective.
