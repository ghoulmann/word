# Reversal Advice Response (MTI 0410)

The issuer's response to a Reversal Advice ([[mti-0400]]). Confirms that the reversal was received and processed. Field 039 (Response Code) indicates whether the reversal was accepted.

In practice, reversals are treated as advice messages — the acquirer proceeds with the reversal regardless of the issuer response — but the response is required for reconciliation.
