# By Responsible Role

This view organizes concepts by D12 — the responsible role: who *owns* fixing a concern in a production workflow.

This is distinct from D5 (evaluator: who *detects* it). A grammar checker detects passive voice (evaluator: automated); a line editor owns the decision to fix it (responsible role: line-editor). The same concern may have different detectors and different owners.
