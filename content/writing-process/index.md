# By Writing Process Stage

The writing process is iterative and recursive — writers move between stages, return to earlier stages, and may work on different parts of a document at different stages simultaneously. This view organizes concerns by where they are most active or most usefully addressed.

Two dimensions are relevant here:
- **D7 — Writing stage** (descriptive): which stages is this concern *active or relevant* at?
- **D8 — Address when** (prescriptive): what is the *earliest* stage at which addressing this concern is not premature?

The triage principle — address concerns top-down through layers, not before higher-layer concerns are stable — is explained in [[triage-sequencing]].

---

## [[prewriting|Prewriting]]
*Discovery, planning, audience analysis, purpose definition*

Concerns that must be resolved before drafting begins. Addressing these during prewriting is not premature — it is necessary. Skipping them means all downstream decisions are made against the wrong target.

**address_when = prewriting**:
- [[audience-awareness]] — identify the reader before any other choice is made
- [[hierarchy-of-purpose]] — define what the document must accomplish
- [[genre]] — choose the form-convention contract appropriate for purpose and reader
- [[topic-type]] — determine the Diataxis type of each planned unit
- [[rhetorical-mode]] — establish the primary organizational logic
- [[chunking]] — plan the document architecture for navigation and retrieval
- [[controlled-vocabulary]] — establish canonical terms before drafting begins

---

## [[drafting|Drafting]]
*Getting content on the page; making compositional decisions*

Concerns that are active during drafting but should not block forward progress. Fix gross structural problems; defer mechanical polish.

**Active during drafting (D7)**:
- [[coherence]] — visible from first draft; correct major logic failures but defer fine-tuning
- [[rhetorical-arrangement]] — organizational decisions solidify during drafting
- [[passive-voice]] — appears during drafting; address when revising
- [[nominalization]] — appears during drafting; address when editing
- [[tokenomics]] — relevant in AI-assisted workflows during composing
- [[e-prime]] — if adopted as a drafting discipline

---

## [[revising|Revising]]
*Structural and logical revision; major reorganization*

The stage at which structural concerns should be resolved. Do not proceed to editing until the argument is stable. This is where the triage line between Layer 3 and Layer 4 is drawn.

**address_when = revising**:
- [[coherence]] — establish logical continuity across claims
- [[unity]] — ensure each section serves one purpose
- [[boundary-control]] — remove content that belongs elsewhere
- [[rhetorical-arrangement]] — finalize organizational strategy
- [[informal-fallacy]] — identify and repair logical errors in arguments
- [[ethos-pathos-logos]] — assess whether appeals are balanced and legitimate
- [[cognitive-overload]] — identify passages that impose excessive working memory load

---

## [[peer-review|Peer Review]]
*External perspective: logical check, audience calibration, clarity*

An independent reader checks what the writer cannot — the gaps in explanation that are invisible to the writer who knows the material.

**Active during peer-review**:
- [[coherence]] — external readers identify where the argument breaks for an unfamiliar reader
- [[informal-fallacy]] — external reviewers catch logical errors the writer missed
- [[audience-awareness]] — the peer reviewer is a proxy audience; their confusion is data
- [[ethos-pathos-logos]] — does the text establish credibility and engage a reader who is not already convinced?

---

## [[editing|Editing]]
*Mechanical polish: grammar, style, compression, consistency*

Editing is for Mechanical and Editorial concerns — after structural revision is complete. This is the triage line: structure must be stable before mechanics are worth addressing.

**address_when = editing**:
- [[nominalization]] — word-level compression
- [[passive-voice]] — restore active constructions
- [[lard-factor]] — eliminate deadwood
- [[weak-words]] — replace vague vocabulary with specific language
- [[grammar-syntax]] — correct grammatical errors
- [[punctuation]] — correct and standardize punctuation
- [[orthography]] — spelling and capitalization
- [[readability-metrics]] — compute reading level; verify target is met
- [[cohesion]] — add transitional devices where connections are missing
- [[tone-voice-cadence]] — calibrate register, voice consistency, and rhythm
- [[house-style]] — apply style guide
- [[terminology-consistency]] — audit term uniformity
- [[information-design]] — finalize visual hierarchy

---

## [[publishing|Publishing]]
*Preparation for the audience and medium; final checks*

Technical concerns that are irrelevant before commitment. Publishing-stage concerns cannot be addressed earlier because the content and links must be stable first.

**address_when = publishing**:
- [[asset-validity]] — validate all links, images, and embedded resources
- [[localization]] — final cultural and locale adaptation
- [[house-style]] — final style guide compliance check
- [[tokenomics]] — final assessment of AI-pipeline performance (if applicable)
